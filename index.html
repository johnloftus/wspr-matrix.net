WSPR Matrix

Daily WSPR propagation activity by reporter, band, UTC time, local time, and selected global destination areas.

See when propagation paths are active

wspr-matrix is a browser App for viewing daily propagation activity between your region and any global region over a UTC day.

The app runs locally in your browser on any operating system - including Windows, Linux, Apple, Android and mobile phones. No installation and no server required.
